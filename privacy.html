JARVIS Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

JARVIS is a privately operated AI assistant intended for use by its authorized operator and explicitly authorized users. This policy describes its intended handling of Google user data. It should be reviewed against the deployed configuration before publication.

Data accessed

When authorized through Google OAuth, JARVIS may access Google Calendar data (including calendars, events and related scheduling information) and Gmail data (including message metadata, message content and attachments), within the scopes granted by the account holder.

How data is used

Google user data is used to fulfill authorized requests, such as reading calendar events, managing events and reminders, reading email, and preparing or sending owner-authorized email communications. JARVIS does not provide a public account registration service.

Storage and security

JARVIS operates in a privately managed environment. Its implementation uses authorization controls and stores operational records and credentials needed to provide authorized functions. Retention, backups, external processing and access protections depend on the deployed configuration and should be verified by the operator before this policy is published.

Sharing and external processing

Google user data may be transmitted to Google APIs to perform requested operations. Depending on enabled features and configuration, user-request content or relevant data may be processed by external AI or messaging providers to fulfill authorized functions. The operator should document those providers and the applicable data handling before publishing this policy. JARVIS does not offer Google user data for sale.

Retention and deletion

Operational data may be retained to support reminders, security, auditing and recovery. Retention and deletion are governed by the deployed system's configuration and operator procedures. Authorized users may contact the operator to request information about their data or its deletion, subject to operational and legal requirements.

Revoking Google access

The Google account holder can revoke JARVIS's Google permissions at Google Account third-party connections. Revocation stops future authorized access using the revoked grant, but does not itself erase previously stored operational data.

Contact

For questions about this policy or Google user data, contact the support email displayed on JARVIS's Google OAuth consent screen.
