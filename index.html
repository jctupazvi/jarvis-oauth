JARVIS

A privately operated AI assistant. Not a public sign-up service.

About

JARVIS is a personal AI assistant developed for use by its authorized operator. It supports voice and messaging interactions and can connect to Google services when the operator grants permission.

Google integrations

With authorization, JARVIS can read calendar information and assist with calendar events and reminders. It can also access email and assist with owner-authorized email operations. Access is limited by the permissions granted during Google sign-in and by JARVIS's own authorization controls.

Access

JARVIS is not offered as a public application. Access to the assistant and its integrations is restricted to authorized users.

Privacy

Read the JARVIS Privacy Policy for information about Google user data, storage, sharing, and revoking access.
